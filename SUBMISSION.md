### Short description

Ione’s lantern came back without her. Ring Bellwick’s bell, cross into its twilight twin, and use forgotten instruments to follow her trail through two worlds.

### Long description

In _Veilbound: Echoes of the Hollow Crown_, Rowan inherits the missing bellwarden’s lantern and follows her trail from the orchards of Aurelia to the Gloam. Roads, cottages and landmarks recur across the veil, but their paths and inhabitants change. A Gale Disc reaches a lock across a flooded room, a chime calls up stepping stones, and a lens reveals paths hidden in plain sight. Each of the ten dungeons asks Rowan to bring its instrument into the guardian fight.

_The First Crossing_ is a short playable chapter. Ring the village bell, follow the answer into a drowned choir, and turn the guardian’s own song into an opening. Choose **Full journey** on the chapter’s title screen to reach the campaign.

### Jury controls

Move with **WASD** or the arrow keys. **F** interacts. **J** strikes, hold **K** to guard, and press **Space** to dodge. **Q** throws the Gale Disc and **E** rings the Echo Chime in the chapter’s default loadout. **Esc** pauses. The chapter keeps session checkpoints. The full campaign uses browser saves and supports save export.

### Generative AI disclosure

OpenAI image generation was used to create painted backdrops, sprites, textures, and interface artwork. The Bellwick and Hushwick concept supplied for the project guided the palette and composition. Generative AI also assisted programming, game design iteration, and writing. The game renderer assembles and animates the artwork. Music and effects are synthesized in the browser with Web Audio code. The trailer uses gameplay capture and the game’s audio. The documented visual assets and their prompts are in the repository.
